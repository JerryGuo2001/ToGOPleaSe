Choose your next move

  • Move to explore your surroundings.
  • Action to interact and discover.
  • Bag to use the items you carry.

Choose a category, then one of its up to nine options. Use Back to return to the category menu.

Follow your adventure

Read each response in the story window. Scroll there to revisit your earlier choices and their results.

Your inventory stays visible on the right. Your score appears inside the story window.

Ready to explore?

You’ll make up to 10 decisions. Each game action uses one decision. Browsing categories, viewing your bag and going Back are free.

At the end, your session data downloads as a CSV file.

Start opens the game in fullscreen when your browser supports it.
